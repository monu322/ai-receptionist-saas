Event-Driven Automation Engine

The intelligence that separates the platform from a basic answering service.

Example: Missed Call Recovery Flow

→

<1s

No Reply?
10 min

Reliability First
BullMQ with Redis persistence. If a worker crashes mid-job, the job retries automatically. No automation is ever silently lost.
Precise Timing
Delayed job execution to the second. Appointment reminders at 48hr, 24hr, 2hr — all scheduled automatically at booking time.
Tenant Isolation
Separate queues per tenant. One busy agency cannot starve another's automation jobs. Fair resource allocation by design.
