AI Business Automation Platform

Workflow 01 — AI voice call
Customer calls the business. The AI receptionist answers in the owner's cloned voice, collects details, answers questions via RAG, books appointments via Cal.com, and logs everything to the CRM — zero human involvement required.
💬
Workflow 02 — Omnichannel chat
WhatsApp, Instagram DM, SMS, and website chat all feed into one unified inbox. The AI handles conversations, answers questions, and books appointments across every channel. Flow B covers proactive broadcast campaigns.
📤
Workflow 03 — Outbound sales
AI dials leads from a configured campaign, qualifies them, handles objections, and books meetings. Non-converters are automatically enrolled in a multi-touch follow-up sequence across SMS, email, and another call attempt.

System design
Technical architecture
Six-layer architecture built as a multi-tenant SaaS from day one. Each business is an isolated tenant with its own knowledge base, voice config, CRM connection, and billing record. The API-first design enables white-label reselling.

Workflow 01
AI voice call
End-to-end flow from when a customer dials the business, through the AI receptionist handling the call, to CRM update and staff notification. Flow B covers missed call recovery — the automated SMS follow-up sequence that fires when no one answers.
Workflow 02
Omnichannel chat
WhatsApp, Instagram DM, SMS, and website chat all feed into one unified pipeline and inbox. Flow B covers proactive broadcast campaigns and automated re-engagement sequences — turning cold leads into active conversations.
Workflow 03
Outbound sales
AI dials leads from a configured campaign list, qualifies them in natural conversation, handles objections, and books meetings or live transfers. Non-converters are automatically enrolled in a multi-touch SMS, email, and call follow-up sequence.
